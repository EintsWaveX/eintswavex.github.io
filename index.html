RAMCES

Asset management and warehouse control for PT KAI's Track & Bridge Division

A full-stack platform tracking light work equipment across the national rail network: gensets, drills, rail grinding machines, track geometry trolleys. It covers registration, field condition reporting, calibration, inter-region transfer, spare-part consumption, and write-off, with role-scoped access so a regional admin sees their region and head office sees everything.

I built the WebSocket live-update layer, JWT authentication across five region-scoped roles, rate limiting, and the response-compression middleware that cuts a 470 KB payload by roughly 8:1. I also refactored the backend from a single 5,900-line file into eleven domain modules holding a strict no-cyclic-imports rule.

SkyNES CPU verified

A Nintendo Entertainment System emulator written from scratch in C++. The 6502 CPU core is verified byte for byte against the nestest golden reference trace across all 8,990 instructions, comparing every register, flag, and cycle count at each step. The PPU renders background tiles; sprites, full scroll, and on-device video output are still in progress.

Smart Rack Monitoring Hardware

Both firmware halves of a dual-ESP32 industrial rack monitor, built as a capstone project. An ESP32-S3 drives a 480 by 320 LVGL touchscreen dashboard; a sensor node reads four load cells, one per rack footing, plus an inertial measurement unit over UART. Loads above 200 kg are resolved to within 1 kg per cell, roughly 4 kg across the rack.

  • C++
  • LVGL
  • HX711
  • UART

Intelligent Waste Sorting Hardware

A lightweight convolutional network, quantized to eight-bit integers and run on an ESP32-CAM, classifies plastic, paper, and metal from a live camera feed in 10 to 150 ms at 85 to 89 percent accuracy. A servo platform then sorts the item, and device state syncs to a companion Android app.

  • TensorFlow
  • TFLite Micro
  • ESP32-CAM
  • Android

PID Climate Control Hardware

A closed-loop temperature and humidity controller for a humidified enclosure. A reverse-acting PID loop modulates a fan blower against a water pump to hold setpoints across a 24 to 32 degree range, with a touchscreen interface graphing temperature, humidity, and controller output over a rolling history.

  • C++
  • PID
  • DHT22
  • TFT
