RAMCES

Asset management & warehouse control for PT KAI Track & Bridge Division

A full-stack railway platform tracking light work equipment across the national rail network: gensets, drills, rail grinding machines, track geometry trolleys. Deployed against PT KAI's real fleet, 1,121 assets across 104 equipment types and 273 locations in 16 regions nationwide. Covers registration, field condition reporting, calibration, inter-region transfers, spare-part ledger, and write-off, with role-scoped access so regional admins manage their territory and head office sees everything.

Engineered the WebSocket live-update layer, JWT authentication across five region-scoped roles, and a durable Postgres rate limiter with progressive CAPTCHA. Response-compression middleware cuts a 470 KB JavaScript and 383 KB HTML payload by roughly 8:1. Refactored the backend from a single 5,900-line file into eleven domain modules under a no-cyclic-imports rule, and built parity tests keeping the Python and JavaScript filters synchronized. 153 commits, in a team of three.

Shipped with the handover the client actually needs: an architecture and data-model reference, a production runbook, a security note, a backup and restore procedure, and a 49-screenshot operator manual in Indonesian for the division's own staff. Read the documentation set.

SkyNES

CPU Verified

Jun 2026 - Present / personal project

A Nintendo Entertainment System emulator written from scratch in C++, covering the 6502 CPU, the PPU, and cartridge and mapper handling. The CPU core is verified byte-for-byte against the nestest golden reference trace across all 8,990 instructions, comparing every register, flag, and cycle count at each step. The core logic runs end to end on the desktop test harness today. Porting it to a custom ESP32-S3 handheld with a 4.0 inch TFT is underway, and hardware bring-up (display wiring) is still in progress.

Intelligent Waste Sorting

TinyML

Nov 2025 - Dec 2025 / team of three

A lightweight convolutional network quantized to 8-bit integers and executed on an ESP32-CAM. Classifies plastic, paper, and metal from a live camera feed in 10 to 150 ms, at 85 to 89 percent accuracy across four input resolutions, reaching 91 percent on plastic and 96 percent on paper. A servo-driven rotating platform then sorts the item into its bin, with device state synced to a companion Android app at 50 to 150 ms latency. Owned the model, the firmware, and the app.

PID Climate Control

Control Loop

Apr 2025 - Jun 2025 / joint coursework project

A closed-loop temperature and humidity controller for a humidified enclosure. A DHT22 feeds a reverse-acting PID loop (Kp 100, Ki 0.1, Kd 5.0) that modulates a fan blower on 25 kHz PWM against a water pump on 1 kHz PWM, holding setpoints across a 24 to 32 degree Celsius range. Built the 3.2 inch TFT touch interface, including a rolling 210-sample graph of temperature, humidity, and PID output.
